Kitchens

We design and build stunning kitchens tailored to your style and needs. Whether you're after a sleek modern space or a classic, timeless look, our expert team delivers high-quality craftsmanship and functionality.

🛁

Bathrooms

From luxurious en-suites to practical family bathrooms, Refit delivers beautifully designed spaces that combine style with functionality. We handle everything from tiling to fixtures and lighting.

Extensions

Expand your living space with a seamless home extension. Whether you need a bigger kitchen or a new living area, we provide expertly crafted extensions designed to enhance your home's value and usability.

Maximize your home's potential with a bespoke loft conversion. Whether for an extra bedroom, office, or living space, we transform underused lofts into stylish, functional areas.

Restorations

Bring your home's character back to life with expert restoration services. We preserve and enhance original details while ensuring modern durability, creating a perfect balance.

External Works

From driveways and patios to fencing and brickwork, we enhance your home's exterior with durable, high-quality craftsmanship designed to improve curb appeal.

We primarily serve London and surrounding areas, but depending on the project, we may be able to travel further. Get in touch to discuss your location and project needs.

Project timelines vary depending on the scope of work. A kitchen or bathroom renovation can take a few weeks, while larger projects like extensions may take several months.

Yes! We offer free, no-obligation quotes. After an initial consultation, we'll assess your project needs and provide a detailed estimate.
